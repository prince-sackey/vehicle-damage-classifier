# Vehicle Damage Classification for Insurance Claims

A 6-class image classifier that identifies vehicle damage type — crack, dent, glass shatter,
lamp broken, scratch, or tire flat — the kind of triage step an insurer could use to route or
pre-assess a claim from a submitted photo.

Two models are trained and compared:
- **Baseline CNN** — a small convolutional network trained from scratch.
- **MobileNetV2 (transfer learning)** — an ImageNet-pretrained backbone with a new classification head.

## Results

MobileNetV2 reached **84.2% accuracy** on a held-out test set of 1,088 images, ahead of the baseline
CNN's ~74% validation accuracy. This held up across a clean, full rerun of the notebook (84.2% vs.
84.0% on the first run), confirming the result is stable rather than a one-off.

| Class | Precision | Recall | F1 | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glass shatter | 0.98 | 0.94 | 0.96 | 190 |
| Tire flat | 0.95 | 0.93 | 0.94 | 85 |
| Scratch | 0.82 | 0.82 | 0.82 | 335 |
| Dent | 0.76 | 0.86 | 0.81 | 310 |
| Lamp broken | 0.85 | 0.72 | 0.78 | 145 |
| Crack | 0.83 | 0.43 | 0.57 | 23 |

Glass shatter and tire flat are classified almost perfectly. Crack is the weak spot (recall 0.43),
most likely due to having far fewer training examples (23 in test vs. 300+ for dent/scratch) rather
than being inherently harder to detect — more labeled crack images would likely close most of this
gap. See the notebook's confusion matrix for exactly which classes get mixed up.
